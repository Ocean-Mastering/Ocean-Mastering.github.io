# Migrated Ocean Mastering media

These files were migrated from the existing Ocean Mastering website at `https://www.ocean-mastering.com/` at the owner's direction on 2026-08-18. They are now hosted locally so the replacement site does not depend on Wix or the former third-party audio player at runtime.

## Audio

`assets/audio/` contains the ten masters exposed by the former embedded player:

1. City of the Violet Crown — JFI
2. AC Current — Randomonium Studios
3. Flat Top Mash — Origami Pigeon
4. You Keep Going — Mood Craft
5. Perfect — JFI
6. Set the World On Fire — Ferrairi Tzar
7. Jazzify — Jonathan Boyle
8. Chris Phaze — The Game On
9. Zincon — HVB Music Group
10. Like Bubble Gum — Where Is Lulu

Original host: `https://msstorage.bestfreecdn.com/storage/uploadcare/images/`

## Founder and studio photography

`assets/studio/` contains the J Fi founder portrait plus the six source-site studio images: console, tube compression, reference monitors, digital workstations, acoustic treatment, and A/D conversion.

Original host: `https://static.wixstatic.com/media/`

## Credits

`assets/credits/` contains all sixteen cover images from the former site's previous-projects gallery. The replacement renders these as an accessible interactive credits wall rather than reproducing the Wix gallery.

Original host: `https://static.wixstatic.com/media/`

`assets/player/ocean-wave-cover.png` is the transparent wave artwork exposed by the source site. It has been placed inside a newly authored Caribbean/deep-ocean interface; the former player's markup, styling, scripts, logo, and green/teal color scheme were not migrated.
